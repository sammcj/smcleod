Sheep wander over your windows after a few minutes without input (or leaves fall, if you pick Leaves in the [Control panel](/control-panel/?pane=system)), but never while a post is open in the reader. Choosing Screen saver from the menu, or typing `screensaver` in the [Terminal](/terminal/), starts it straight away. Move the mouse, tap or press a key to come back. Without JavaScript there is no screen saver.
